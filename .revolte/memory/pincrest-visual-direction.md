Pincrest is being redesigned around image-led discovery, effortless collections, and creator exploration, with all three equally important. The user specifically ruled out green; use a Pinterest-inspired red accent with neutral surfaces. Preserve current flows and visualize all existing app screens consistently. Why it matters: palette is a direct user constraint. How to apply: keep green out of the shared theme and touched surfaces; use red for brand/primary actions, neutral colors for content, and separate danger semantics.
